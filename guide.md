# A beginner's route through Ashen Empires

From the beach of Valinor to about level 30, with what to do, in which order, and why: for each of the three
towns a new character can go to after Valinor (Krog, Silvest, Whisperdale), and what comes after.

It is written for somebody who has never played. If you only want the order, read
[The route in one page](#the-route-in-one-page) and the page of your town.

## How far to trust this

The guide was put together in October 2026 from six sources, and they are not equally sure.

| Source | What it is | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| The game itself | Its quest list and the text of every stage, its items and creatures, where creatures roam | Which quests exist, kill counts, creature levels, what a reward item does and from which level it can be worn |
| [AE Guides](https://bofur24.github.io/AEGuides) | A player's walk through Valinor, the three starting regions and the Highlands | Who starts a quest, the steps, gold and experience rewards |
| [Ashen Empires Wiki](https://ashenempires.fandom.com/wiki/Quests) | A page for most quests, the patch notes of 2020 to 2026 | Steps, coordinates, what changed since the guides were written |
| [AE Database](https://www.aedatabase.com) | A players' database: the bestiary, the list of quest rewards | A second opinion on creature levels and on what each reward is, which slot it takes and from which level it is worn |
| The community map | 1,308 markers | Where banks, gates, portals and shops are |
| Our own play | Two characters run through Valinor, one archer played from level 10 to 35 in Whisperdale, with the game's chat logs | What the game says today |

Where they disagree, the game's data wins on what it holds, the newest patch note wins on rules, and our own logs
win on what was seen. Three things follow from that, and you will meet them all through the guide:

- **The level given for a quest is a judgment**, made from the levels of what you fight and the level the reward
  needs. No source states "do this at level 12". Take one level more if you play alone and die easily (a mage),
  one less if you hit hard.
- **Experience numbers are rough.** The guides took theirs at one moment on one server; quest experience grows
  with your level and during a Power Hour. Use them to compare quests, not to plan to the point.
- **Both guides are older than two changes.** In 2025 the way the town patrons reward you was replaced (see
  [The patron](#the-patron-your-main-source-of-levels)), and several creatures were made stronger than the
  guides say. This guide follows the game's data and the patch notes there.

What was held against what:

- Every reward item named here was looked up in the game's own item table. AE Database's list of quest rewards
  agrees with that table on the level requirement of every item the two share.
- Of the 78 kinds of creature named here, the game and AE Database give the same level for all but two: the
  database has the Orc Renegade at 11 and the Orc Renegade Soldier at 16, the game at 20 and 22. The game's
  figures are used; a patch of 2025 changed the renegades.
- Where a creature lives was taken from the game's own table of what roams where, not from AE Database's spawn
  points. The two were not compared quest by quest.
- Not checked against anything: the pictures the wiki uses in place of directions for some quests, and whatever
  the game only says in a talk with somebody we have not spoken to.

Places are given as sextant coordinates, `x,y`, the way the game's Sextant shows them. `y` grows to the south.

What is still unknown is collected at [the end](#what-nobody-could-tell-us), so that nobody takes a guess for a
fact.

| Level | Where | What |

| 1 to 3 | Valinor, in and around town | The welcome quest, one weapon or magic trainer, the no-fight quests for two bags, the level 1 and 2 hunts for your first gear |
| 3 to 7 | Valinor, south-west | "War" as far as the goblins, and "Proof" for the **Totem of the Wolf Pup** on the same goblins |
| 7 to 10 | Valinor, the east | The undead at the gate, Frump the Cook, the cloak and the boots from beyond the gate. Fix your orbs for free. Leave |
| 8 to 10 | Your town | Bind at the spawn gate, align at the Steward, take a patron mission, take every quest in town |
| 10 to 15 | Around your town | The quests that fight level 5 to 12 creatures; always one patron mission running |
| 15 to 20 | The edges of your region | The level 14 to 20 quests; the trainer quests of your weapon or magic; the first pieces of the patron set |
| 20 to 25 | Your region's dungeons, and the two other towns by portal | Put on the level 20 gear you earned; the bosses; the best quests of the other two towns |
| 25 to 30 | Wherever the patron sends you | Patron missions, the last hard quests of the region, then the Highlands |
| 30 to 50 | Highlands, the rest of Krythan, Gulley | See [After your starting region](#after-your-starting-region) |

Five things matter more than any single quest:

1. **Get the Totem of the Wolf Pup before you leave Valinor.** It speeds up your attacks and costs about 25
   goblins. There is a way to get it later, but it is a detour.
2. **Settle your orbs on Valinor.** Resetting them is free and unlimited there, and free only once more after it.
3. **Align with the town that fits your main attribute**, not simply the one you arrive in.
4. **Keep a patron mission running at all times** once you are on the mainland. It is the best experience in the
   game below level 50 and it pays for your armor.
5. **You are not tied to your town.** A free portal network joins the three towns, and each has a few quests that
   are worth the trip for anyone.

From the game's own Help Menu:

| Attribute | What it does |
|---|---|
| Strength | Damage of melee and ranged attacks, how much you can carry |
| Dexterity | Hitting and not being hit. Half your Dexterity is added to your armor |
| Intelligence | Chance to cast, spell damage, stamina |
| Constitution | Health, regeneration, stamina |

Each level gives you orbs to put into these. Gear has requirements, and they are checked against your **base**
orbs: bonuses from other gear do not count. Armor asks for a character level, a weapon asks for a level in its
own weapon skill.

The Help Menu names four builds. They are a good place to start:

| Build | Orbs | Town to align with |
|---|---|---|

| Versatile Warrior | Dexterity first, then Strength | Whisperdale |
| Classic Mage | Intelligence and Constitution | Silvest |
| Versatile Mage | Intelligence and Dexterity | Silvest |
| Archer (not named in the Help Menu) | Dexterity and Strength | Whisperdale |

### Which town

When you leave Valinor, the Island Steward asks where you want to go. Two different choices hide behind that
question, and the game does not make it obvious:

- **Where you arrive.** This only decides which town you step into. It can be undone by walking through a portal.
- **Which town you align with.** You do this afterwards, at the Steward next to the town's spawn gate. It gives
  one extra orb in that town's attribute and opens that town's patron.

| Town | People | Extra orb | Patron | Patron set gives |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Krog | Orcs | +1 Strength | Lord Targon | +40 Str, +14 Con, +295 health |
| Silvest | Humans | +1 Intelligence | Lord Aelfwyn | +40 Int, +14 Str, +295 health |
| Whisperdale | Night elves | +1 Dexterity | Lord Vezryl | +40 Dex, +14 Str, +295 health |

**Align by your main attribute, whatever your race.** The extra orb is the only way to reach 101 in an attribute,
and some of the last gear in the game asks for 101 Strength, 101 Intelligence or 101 Dexterity. That is a long
way off, and the choice can be undone once for free (at Lotor's Steward in Lotor's Castle; after that it costs 60
tokens from the store). The more immediate reason is the patron set: you will wear it from about level 15 to 45,
and you want the one that raises your own attribute.

Race does not lock you out: our human archer aligned with Whisperdale and was given the patron's missions and
the archer quests there.

If you are unsure, go to the town of your build and align there. If you arrived somewhere else by mistake, do not
align; take the portal to the right town first (see [Travel](#travel)).

## Valinor, level 1 to 10

Valinor is the island every character starts on. Three rules shape everything you do there:

- Your character stops at **level 10** and every skill at **level 15**.
- Dying costs nothing.
- **You can leave whenever you like, and never come back.**

So the aim is not to get out fast. It is to leave at level 9 or 10 with the totem, the bags, the gear that is
still good on the mainland, and your orbs where you want them.

One trap: the wiki says quests stop paying experience once you are level 10 ("you have learned all you can on
Valinor"). Hand quests in as you go, do not save them up.

### Trainers: pick your weapon, take more if you like

In the hall around Sage Haritod stand fourteen trainers. Each gives you a starting weapon and a quest in seven
steps: gain 500, 700, 900, 1,100, 1,500, 3,000 and 5,000 experience **in that skill**, returning after each for a
reward. Since 2024 you can run several of these at once.

| You are | Take | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Warrior | Large Blades, or Polearms | The Sword of the Island Guard (7-18) is the best one-handed reward; the Island Guard Lance (7-20) hits hardest |
| Archer | Bows | Island Archers Bow (5-15, fast), and 100 free arrows at every step |
| Mage | Body first, then the other three schools | Body is the heal. Each school gives 8 runes, a sash (+2 to the school) and an amulet (+3). Having all four at 15 is what the Silvest mage quests ask for later |
| Anyone | Farming | Its last step gives a **Storage Crate** (24 slots). It is the only trade whose reward we know to be worth it |

Two warnings. A skill that reaches 15 before the last step cannot finish its training, so do not pour experience
into a skill you are still being trained in. And never spend experience pool on a skill your character has no
level in at all: on our runs the server ended the session each time.

The magic trainers are down the stone stairs at 763,118; the weapon trainers on the right side of the hall, the
trade trainers on the left.

### Before you step through the portal

- [ ] The **Totem of the Wolf Pup** is in your backpack.
- [ ] Your orbs are where your build wants them. The reset is the "Valinor Orb Reset" at 717,99: free, as often
      as you like. The mainland gives you one more free reset and no others.
- [ ] You carry the Large Knapsack, the Old Worn Pack and, if you farmed, the Storage Crate.
- [ ] You wear Frump's Amulet, the Zombie Skin Cloak, the Marsh Soaked Boots, the Helm of the Island Guard.
      Mages keep the sash and amulet of their school. All of these outlast the first mainland levels.
- [ ] What you do not carry is in the bank (743,99). The bank is the same everywhere in the world.
- [ ] You know which town you align with.

Then speak to the Island Steward by the portal in the north-east of town (777,102), name your town, answer yes,
and walk through.

## Your first ten minutes on the mainland

The same in all three towns:

1. **Click the spawn gate.** That is where you come back to life from now on.
2. **Align at the Steward** who stands next to the gate, if this is the town of your build. You get the extra
   orb, a first batch of experience, and the patron will talk to you.
3. **Go to the patron** in the town's hall and take a mission.
4. **Walk the town and take every quest** before you go out. Most can run side by side, and many share a road.
5. **Find the bank**, and the portal to Lotor's Summer Palace outside town. You will want both later.

What changes on the mainland is death. Away from the towns' safe zones, dying wears down your gear for good, and
on some servers your backpack stays in a grave where you fell. Most of the land around each starting town has
been a safe zone since 2022, so the first levels are forgiving. See [Death](#death).

## The patron: your main source of levels

Each town's lord gives missions to those aligned with the town: "slay 20 of this creature". The creature is
picked for your level, somewhere near town. You can repeat them without end.

They are worth more than any other single thing you can do below level 50. Our archer went from level 10 to 35 in
four days of play, mostly on missions. What one mission paid, as experience **and the same again as experience
pool**:

| Level | Experience per mission |
|---|---|
| 16 to 18 | about 31,000 |
| 19 | about 49,000 |
| 23 to 27 | about 76,000 to 79,000 |
| 28 to 29 | about 106,000 to 118,000 |
| 30 to 34 | about 119,000 to 128,000 |
| 35 | about 177,000 |

Compare that with a one-off quest of the same levels, which pays 10,000 to 40,000 once. The one-off quests are
for gear, gold, bags and access. The missions are for levels.

Each mission also gives one **coin** of the town (Orcish Coin in Krog, Krythan Coin in Silvest, Soluri Coin in
Whisperdale), some faction with the town, and now and then a Scroll of Return to it.

### The patron set, and how you get it now

**Both guides are out of date here.** They describe a Lady in another town (Maraket, Varg, the Vezryl estate)
who hands you a piece of armor each time your faction passes a threshold. Since a patch in 2025:

- The Lady stands **next to her Lord**, in the town's own hall, and keeps a shop.
- **You buy the pieces with the coins** the missions give.
- The shop also sells a **Stone** of the town, which teleports you to the Lord.

This is confirmed three ways: the patch notes say so, the game's item table holds the three coins and three
stones, and our archer's log shows one coin for each of 21 missions.

The ten pieces are the same in all three towns except for the main attribute (Strength in Krog, Intelligence in
Silvest, Dexterity in Whisperdale) and the second one (Constitution in Krog, Strength in the other two). **None of
them has a level requirement.**

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Necklace | +1 | +1 | +25 | 2 | 1 |
| Ring | +2 | +1 | +25 | 4 | 2 |
| Gloves (Bracers in Whisperdale) | +3 | +1 | +15 | 15 | 3 |
| Boots | +3 | +2 | +25 | 25 | 4 |
| Cloak | +5 | +2 | +35 | 15 | 4 |
| Helm | +5 | +2 | +25 | 20 | 5 |

**The prices are our reading, not a stated fact.** They are the "cost" the game's item table gives each piece,

gate what the shop will sell you.

## Krog and the Black Swamp

The orcs' town, for Strength builds. Lord Targon is in the hall at 3076,3763, with the Lady of Krog's shop next
to him; the spawn gate and the Krog Steward are at 3095,3754. South of Krog lies Maraket (bank at 3076,4007),
north-west Samad, north the road to Dawat.

Krog is the kindest of the three arrivals: six quests can be done at level 8 to 11 on one loop down the road to
Maraket, three of them without a fight, and together they give two pieces of armor, a 16-slot bag and more
experience than everything on Valinor.

### The order

**Level 8 to 11: one loop to Maraket**

1. In town: align, take a mission, do Leonard is Lost, and take Tree Trouble, Formidable Formicidae, Protect
   Krog!, Reclaiming What's Mine and Sting Operation.
2. Go into the sewer south-west of town (3019,3816) to Prudge for Great Disrepair, then up to Aunt Rekant, above
   the provisioner (3092,3789).
3. Walk the road south: the Cedar tree by the sewer entrance (2974,3852), the 40 Fire Ants at 3095,3865, the
   Peach tree south of the ants (3091,3903), the Oak tree west of the path before Maraket (3057,3954).
4. In Maraket: Dander in the magic shop (3082,4006), then Unga in the middle of town, Teppu by the big campfire
   in the north-east, and Unga again. Use the bank.
5. On the way back, Ivogur's mine (2962,3912): crate 3 to the north-west first, back to the entrance, then 5 and
   4 to the south-west, down the trap door beside 4 for 1 and 2. Kill a bat, a snake and one kobold for Warrog
   wherever you meet them.
6. In Krog: hand in to Singaford, Haburt, Ivogur (then open his crate south of where he stands, between the
   mountain and the nearest building), Warrog; then Rekant and Wuhn, who walks outside the provisioner. Wuhn gives
   you the order of the levers.

Two warnings for the loop. **Large Fire Ants (level 25) stand among the Fire Ants** and do not count; pull the
small ones away. And when you tell Teppu about Unga, the answer "Unga is already married" pays 1,000 gold, the
other 100.

**Level 10 to 14: the levers and the scorpions**

- **Great Disrepair, the levers.** On the bottom layer of the sewer, pull them in the order **A, F, B, D, E, C**.
  A wrong pull resets them all. If you fall into a hole, the ladder out comes up at lever F. Lever C is up the
  ladder after E. Run past what lives down there instead of fighting it. The Large Knapsack and the experience
  make this the best single quest of the region.
- **Sting Operation, parts one and two.** The scorpion pit is north of town (hole at 3131,3660). Find the bones
  and leave; **do not fight Schlag yet**. Then 20 Scorpion Stings from Scorpions (level 5). The 30 healing potions
  you get carry you through the next ten levels.
- **Hole of Tears.** Spiders (level 3) around town, cook five legs, bring them to the prisoner.

**Level 12 to 17**

- Missions, and hunting to bring your weapon skill to 15.
- Honor in Battle, if you use a sword (see below).
- Schlag (level 20, 500 to 650 health, poisons, calls scorpions) at about 14 to 16, with the cure and healing
  potions of part two.
- Agon's Hand. The Dragoons for its third hunt are in the swamp north-west of Krog and by Maraket; the wiki
  sends you to an island near Whisperdale for them, which is not needed.

**Level 16 to 22**

- **Agon's Test.** The four Shadow pieces give +7 to every attribute between them, need only level 8, and suit
  any build: the best gear of the region for the level you get it at. Its last step is to attack an Orc Guard
  (level 100). One hit is enough. You will be Wanted and probably killed or jailed, so take your gear off first,
  shoot from range if you can, and run.
- Stick, Jab, Twist and Never Stop at Lake Dresta (the goblins, north-west of Krog), if those are your weapons.
- Payment in Full. Decide before you answer Dunaga: **the second answer gives Dunaga's Cap**, the first gives 500
  faction and no item. Take the cap.
- Swamped. It looks like a starter quest and is not: the Swamp Slugs of its first step are level 27, and the
  steps cannot be reordered.

**Level 20 to 25**

- The last trainer quests, and the Orcish Warrior pieces become wearable at 20.
- The region's one-off quests are finished by about level 22 to 25. Targon's missions go on to 50.

### The order

**Level 8 to 10: around the walls**

1. Align, take a mission, then collect Put Them Down, Nest of Pests, Quite Entranced, Spice It Up, Join Forces,
   Juicy and The Wisdom of Wizards.
2. Put Them Down, then Nest of Pests. The ants only count when they die at the mound.
3. Quite Entranced, the first two parts: buy a foraging stick (175 gold) for the Bloodroot; Scorpions are
   level 5. Kill a Snake with Raglnak's Practice Sword on the way.
4. Spice It Up whenever you want a rest: buy a fishing pole (150 gold). The counter shows nothing until the
   fiftieth fish.

**Level 10 to 13**

5. The Wrong Crowd. Cult Leaders (level 16) walk among the Cultists; leave them. The Plague Toads for Quite
   Entranced are at the same temple.
6. Something's Bugging Sozal. The cave drops a great deal of silk and spider legs; watch your weight.
7. The Lake. Kill one Kobold with the Practice Sword while you are there.
8. Your first walk east to Fort Eastwatch: click its spawn gate if you mean to hunt there, use its bank, take
   Supply Problems, Dalvon's Undead and Gem Thieves for later.

**Level 14 to 18**

9. The Fort, then The Attack. Hothbra is far to the west, past Varg, through land where Orc Renegades of level 20
   and more roam: keep to the road. Before you report to Lowes, be sure you can kill 10 Kobolds in five minutes.
   Failing means starting the quest again.
10. For Those Who Are Brave, in the Silvest Mine north of Cliffside. Be kind to William when you tell him; a rude
    answer may cost you the reward.
11. Join Forces: the Orc Renegade, in the Orc Cave (entrance 1180,1300). It has 300 health and the Practice Sword
    does 1 to 9.

**Level 18 to 25**

12. At 20, hand in The Wisdom of Wizards and put on what you earned.
13. From Fort Eastwatch: Dalvon's Undead and the first step of Gem Thieves side by side.
14. Of Kobolds. The short way in is the ladder in the small building at Karzak Cove (1378,1106), then west to a
    hidden ladder up into the fort. Leave by clicking a candlestick at the gate.
15. Gem Thieves, the Foremen, at 24 to 27.

**Level 25 to 30**

16. Master Blaster (level 40, 1,200 health), with help if you can find it.
17. Women and Ale, from Varg.
18. Juicy and Save the Family Farm, when you travel to Vrethpool.

Stay north of the Dalvon Mine. South of it lies the Valley of the Dead, with creatures of level 27 to 38.

### Fishing

Paezer's line of quests is long and peaceful, and its first step is worth doing for anyone who can fish a
little: the Tackle Box is a 20-slot container that halves the weight of its contents. It needs Fly-casting 5 and
"Spice It Up" from Silvest. Save every fish you catch from the start, since his second step asks for 30 of one
kind, and which kind differs from character to character. After the second quest it needs Fly-casting 30 and
far-off towns.

## After your starting region

All three starting regions run out of new quests between level 25 and 28. The patron still pays well up to 50,
so keep a mission running wherever you go, and use the Stone or a Scroll of Return to hand it in.

### The Highlands, level 15 to 50

North of Krog, on the same land, lie Dawat, Arda, Ethera Palace, the Emporium Abbey and Parian. This is where
most characters go next, and its first quests are worth doing long before you are done at home: they fight
creatures of level 8 to 20 and give gear worn at 10 and 15.

**Getting there.** From Krog walk the road north to Dawat (bank 3093,3377, spawn gate 3094,3363). From the other
towns take the portal to Lotor's Summer Palace and then either the Krog portal and the same road, or the Parian
portal (it comes out at 3415,2919; Parian's spawn gate is at 3469,3025). **The Highlands are not a safe zone**:
a death here wears your gear.

Three of these deserve a word.

- **Friend of the Earth is the best early quest in the game for a mage, and good for anyone.** Nothing else gives
  +3 to every attribute at level 10. The wiki advises the hood or the gloves, since a better cloak comes sooner
  than a better hat.
- **In Hero of Dawat, do not kill Andreev the Quick while you are still on the thieves.** If he is not there
  when his turn comes, clear the thieves and come back in an hour. The kills only count inside the marked area.
- **The Boots of the Traveller have no level requirement.** A level 20 character cannot kill level 40 minotaurs,
  but a friend can, and they are the first boots that make you walk faster.

## Things a beginner has to know

### Travel

::: pictures
wiki Portal.jpg | A granite portal to Lotor's Summer Palace | General_Gameplay
wiki Blueportal.png | A blue portal, for longer jumps | General_Gameplay
wiki Sextant.png | The Sextant shows where you stand | General_Gameplay

:::


- **The portal network.** Outside Krog (3110,3686), Silvest (999,1337), Whisperdale (472,3481), Parian
  (3415,2919) and Josody stands a granite portal to **Lotor's Summer Palace**. In the palace the portals back
  stand together in one block. So any of these towns is two steps from any other, for free, with no waiting on
  the Legends server.
- **Spawn gates.** You come back to life at the last gate you clicked. Click the gate of wherever you are
  hunting, and remember to click your home gate again afterwards.
- **Getting home fast.** A Scroll of Return takes you to its town, three times. The patron hands one out now and
  then. The Stone of your town, from the Lady's shop, takes you to the Lord.
- **On foot** otherwise. Ask a mage for a speed spell; nobody minds.
- **Ships** belong to the Tides of Fate expansion and to much later in the game.

### Death

- You wake at your spawn gate. The place you died is marked on the map with a white gravestone.
- **Around the three starting towns you are in a safe zone**, caves included: as far as the river crossing
  towards Dawat from Krog, the guard gate towards Varg from Silvest, and the river before Darkfell from
  Whisperdale.
- **Outside a safe zone, each death lowers the most health your weapons and armor can have**, by 5 (by 10 on the
  Legends server). At nothing, the item is gone. Only a purchase from the store restores it. Ordinary wear is
  mended with potions, tools or spells.
- **Your backpack.** On the Legends server it stays with you. On the others, outside a no-drop zone, it stays in
  a grave where you fell; after a while anyone can loot it. Go back for it.
- On the Heroes and Ascension servers other players can attack you outside the towns.

So: before something risky far from home, such as the Orc Guard at the end of Agon's Test, take your gear off.

### Orbs

- You start with 7 orbs to place and get 2 with every level. Your race adds one in its own attribute every 20
  levels, up to five.
- The Help Menu's builds, as end points: Classic Warrior 101 Str, 100 Con, 30 Dex; Versatile Warrior 101 Dex,
  100 Str, 30 Con; Classic Mage 101 Int, 100 Con, 30 Dex; Versatile Mage 101 Int, 100 Dex, 30 Con.
- The two sources disagree on the way there. The wiki says: everything into your main attribute until it is 100,
  and no Constitution at first. The Help Menu says: one attribute for damage, and Constitution. A character that
  dies less learns the game faster; a few orbs of Constitution early are not wasted.
- **Requirements look at the orbs you placed.** Bonuses from gear, food and totems do not count.
- Resetting your orbs is free on Valinor as often as you like, and once on the mainland, at Lady Esler in
  Josody. After that it is bought in the store.

### The experience pool

::: pictures

wiki SkillWindow.jpg | The Skills window | Skills
wiki Quest Log.PNG | The quest log | Game_Interface
:::


Quests pay twice: experience for your level, and the same or more again into the **experience pool**. The pool
is experience you hand out yourself, in the Skills window (`K`), to any combat skill with a `+` beside it. It is
how a warrior brings a second weapon to 15 for a trainer, or a mage a school of magic. It cannot go into trades
or sailing.

Two cautions from our own play: a skill needs a first level, earned by use, before the pool can go into it; and
do not pour the pool into a skill a Valinor trainer is still training, or it may pass 15 before he is done.

### Totems

A totem is an item you click for an effect that lasts a while.

- **Totem of the Wolf Pup**: faster attacks, for 30 minutes a click. From "Proof" on Valinor, or "Loyal" in
  Whisperdale.
- **Totem of the Wolf**: the next one up, dropped by dire wolves, wolverines and, since 2026, by hobgoblins,
  ettins and hunters of level 35 to 50.
- The others are made with a trade from an expansion, or belong to much higher levels.

### Trades worth an early look

A skill is learned by using it once, and there is no limit to how many you have. Three pay off early: **Alchemy**
(healing potions from the first level, and the ingredients drop on Valinor), **Leatherworking** (a bag at level
1), and **Carpentry** (arrows, bolts and torches at the bottom, crates later). Fishing has its own line of quests
in Whisperdale. The pool does not help with any of them.

### Lotor, Talazar, or neither

There are two sides in the world and you start on neither. Joining one, or leaving it again, is a quest that
begins in Lotor's Summer Palace. For a new character it changes almost nothing: since 2020 it no longer decides
who you can group with or fight, and what it opens are two dungeons for level 100 and more. Leave it until
somebody you play with gives you a reason.

What does matter early is your **standing**. Attacking guards or townsfolk makes you a criminal, and criminals
come back to life at other gates and are attacked on sight in the ordinary towns. A few towns (Redwake, Grell,
Darushk, Rahura, Meroven) are the other way round and attack characters of good standing.

### VIP, Power Hour, events

- **VIP** is the game's subscription: 10% more experience, four more places in the backpack, a stone that
  returns you to Lotor's Summer Palace, and automatic looting when you are alone. The game is fully playable
  without it.
- A **Power Hour** is an hour of extra experience for the whole server, set off by players. The experience
  figures on the wiki were measured during one, which is one more reason to read them as rough.
- **Quests marked with a golden chalice** are small errands that fail if you log out or talk to somebody else.
  They pay little.
- **Guilds** take new players gladly; joining needs an invitation, nothing more. Founding one is a long quest
  and 250,000 gold.

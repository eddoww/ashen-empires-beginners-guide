    Silvest and the Silvest Valley

    The humans' town, for Intelligence builds, on the island of Krythan. Lord Aelfwyn is in the hall; the spawn gate is at 1127,1338 with the Silvest Steward beside it at 1131,1341; the bank at 1097,1374. North lies Cliffside, east Fort Eastwatch, west (through the guard gate) Varg.

    Silvest pays the most gold early and has the one long quest line written for mages. It is also the most spread out, and the common ground around town holds a Spider Queen (level 15) among the bats and snakes. If something kills you at level 9 that you did not see coming, that was probably her.

    The order

    Level 8 to 10: around the walls

    1. Align, take a mission, then collect Put Them Down, Nest of Pests, Quite Entranced, Spice It Up, Join Forces, Juicy and The Wisdom of Wizards.
    2. Put Them Down, then Nest of Pests. The ants only count when they die at the mound.
    3. Quite Entranced, the first two parts: buy a foraging stick (175 gold) for the Bloodroot; Scorpions are level 5. Kill a Snake with Raglnak's Practice Sword on the way.
    4. Spice It Up whenever you want a rest: buy a fishing pole (150 gold). The counter shows nothing until the fiftieth fish.

    Level 10 to 13

    1. The Wrong Crowd. Cult Leaders (level 16) walk among the Cultists; leave them. The Plague Toads for Quite Entranced are at the same temple.
    2. Something's Bugging Sozal. The cave drops a great deal of silk and spider legs; watch your weight.
    3. The Lake. Kill one Kobold with the Practice Sword while you are there.
    4. Your first walk east to Fort Eastwatch: click its spawn gate if you mean to hunt there, use its bank, take Supply Problems, Dalvon's Undead and Gem Thieves for later.

    Level 14 to 18

    1. The Fort, then The Attack. Hothbra is far to the west, past Varg, through land where Orc Renegades of level 20 and more roam: keep to the road. Before you report to Lowes, be sure you can kill 10 Kobolds in five minutes. Failing means starting the quest again.
    2. For Those Who Are Brave, in the Silvest Mine north of Cliffside. Be kind to William when you tell him; a rude answer may cost you the reward.
    3. Join Forces: the Orc Renegade, in the Orc Cave (entrance 1180,1300). It has 300 health and the Practice Sword does 1 to 9.

    Level 18 to 25

    1. At 20, hand in The Wisdom of Wizards and put on what you earned.
    2. From Fort Eastwatch: Dalvon's Undead and the first step of Gem Thieves side by side.
    3. Of Kobolds. The short way in is the ladder in the small building at Karzak Cove (1378,1106), then west to a hidden ladder up into the fort. Leave by clicking a candlestick at the gate.
    4. Gem Thieves, the Foremen, at 24 to 27.

    Level 25 to 30

    1. Master Blaster (level 40, 1,200 health), with help if you can find it.
    2. Women and Ale, from Varg.
    3. Juicy and Save the Family Farm, when you travel to Vrethpool.

    Stay north of the Dalvon Mine. South of it lies the Valley of the Dead, with creatures of level 27 to 38.

    Gem Thieves

    The wiki calls this one "a must do", and the rewards bear it out:

    Step Kill Reward
    On taking it 1,000 gold
    1 50 Kobold Miners (21) 10 Diamonds
    2 50 Kobold Foremen (27), deeper down the central ladder Gemstone of Dalvon (+3 Str, +3 Int, +3 Dex, no requirement)
    3 Master Blaster (40), in the southernmost part, on a timer Dalvon Miner's Ring (for smiths and miners) and one of three Dalvon Steel pieces

    Do not kill Master Blaster before his step. And a mage should know that all three Dalvon Steel pieces need 25 Strength.

    Farming, if you want a second trade

    Stratton in the farm shop (1079,1380) starts "Fine Land", the first of 25 farming quests that end at level 100. The first three (Fine Land, Black Gold, Honest Work) are cheap, give a Storage Crate and ten growth potions, and can be done from about level 15, since Honest Work waits for The Attack. They reward farming skills, not levels. After those it turns expensive (about 100,000 gold and level 44 fights). Do the first three if farming interests you; otherwise leave it.

    Leaving Silvest

    • The portal to Lotor's Summer Palace is the granite portal west of town at 999,1337. From the palace, portals go to Krog and Whisperdale.
    • The Silvest Valley's own quests are done by about level 25 to 28. The rest of Krythan (Varg's pirates, Vrethpool, the wasps near Hothbra) carries on to about 35.
    • Keep away from New Korelth (341,1439) in the south-west: it is the stronghold of the other side.

    What all the quests ask of you

    This page is for planning a character a long way ahead. It puts together what every quest in the game asks before it lets you start or finish it: your level, your skills, kill ranks, expansions, faction, party and gold. It was compiled in November 2026 from all 217 quest pages of the wiki, with the patch notes of 2023 to 2026 where they changed a requirement. It is the wiki's word, not tested in play, and where the wiki is out of date or disagrees with itself the page says so.

    All of it in one table

    To finish every quest the wiki describes, one character needs at least:

    Expansions All three Many; Fine Land and Fishing for Friends need all three by their end

    Harvesting, Foraging, Milling, Mining and Identification are needed as well, at levels no source gives. Faction has to be changed several times, and a few quests need a party of six to nine.

    Expansions, store items and events

    • Talazar's Revenge, Sands of Creation and Tides of Fate: every quest in their lands needs them. Two quests in the old world need Talazar's Revenge too (Crypt of Lotor and The Dig Went Bad), and Nautical Knowhow needs both Talazar's Revenge and Tides of Fate. See The expansions.
    • A pass to the Broken Finger Dungeon from the store, for its four quests: 60 Hounds of Cerebus, 100 War Giants, 200 Dark Guardians, Lord Cerebus.
    • A Lair of Minos Access Ticket, for Celis's Secret.
    • A fishing tournament trophy, for Fishing for Friends: win one (event tickets from the store), or buy one from the Fishing Event Judge for 10 million gold.
    • The Hell Event, the only time Return to Hell is offered.

    Faction and standing

    Faction (Lotorian, Talazarian, Neutral) and standing (how good or evil your name is) are different things, and the quests ask for both.

    Quest Faction
    Lotorian faction quest, In Service to the Great One (Talazarian)Neutral
    To Take No Side (back to Neutral)Lotorian or Talazarian
    Of a Venomous SpiritTalazarian
    Crypt of LotorNeutral or Talazarian
    Royal RelicLotorian or Neutral

    To do all of them, change faction like this: Neutral, then Talazarian (do Of a Venomous Spirit), then Neutral again with To Take No Side, then Lotorian. Each change needs an hour in the faction you are leaving.

    Standing. Neutral or worse to walk into Redwake, Rahura, Meroven and the evil town of Darushk (Stop the Ocean Motion) without the guards attacking you; Midnight Warrior and Dirty Work start in Rahura. Riches puts you in jail several times. For My Pleasure needs you evil. The way down is to join the temple in Lopal for 25,000 gold, as often as it takes.

    Town. The three patron sets need you aligned with each town in turn. The first change is free, at Lotor's Steward in Lotor's Castle; after that it is 60 tokens.

    Where the sources disagree

    • Skinning for Titans. Latest Fascination says 50 Skinning; Toxic Blood says 85 for the Titan Scorpions that count toward it.
    • Fly-casting for Fishing for Friends. The wiki gives a level for its first seven parts only, ending at 60. The patch notes of 2024 say the line has "one quest every 10 levels" up to 90 and a master quest for 90 and over, so 90 is taken here.
    • The elf trainers. The wiki's page asks for 12 to 20 in bows, crossbows or thrown weapons; the patch of 2025 says 10.
    • Fine Land. One version says 10 Harvesting and 20 Planting to start, the newer one 30 between the two.

    Worth a portal trip, whichever town you chose

    The three towns are a minute apart: walk to the granite portal outside your town, and in Lotor's Summer Palace take the portal of the town you want (see Travel). These are the quests of the other towns worth that trip. As far as any source says, none of them asks you to be aligned with the town; we have only tried this ourselves in Whisperdale.

    Level Town Quest Why
    8 Whisperdale Loyal The Totem of the Wolf Pup, if you left Valinor without it. Level 6 creatures, a short walk from the portal
    8 to 11 Krog Reclaiming What's Mine A level 10 helm, 1,500 gold and a large reward of experience for finding five crates
    8 to 11 Krog Tree Trouble For mages: +7 Int +5 Dex on the legs at level 10, for clicking three trees
    8 to 11 Krog Formidable Formicidae 40 level 8 ants for one of the larger experience rewards of the early game
    8 to 12 Silvest Spice It Up No fighting; it opens the Tackle Box in Whisperdale
    8 to 12 Silvest Put Them Down, Nest of Pests 3,500 gold for level 6 and 8 creatures
    10 to 14 Krog Great Disrepair A second 16-slot knapsack
    10 to 12 Silvest Something's Bugging Sozal A ring with +1 to everything and no requirement
    10 to 14 Whisperdale Astari Supplies +3 Dex +3 Con at level 7 (if it is open to you)
    9 to 12 Krog Sting Operation, to its second part 30 Potions of Greater Healing
    12 to 15 Whisperdale Noisy Neighbors A level 15 chest piece without a fight
    16 to 20 Krog Agon's Hand, Agon's Test Four pieces with +1 or +2 to every attribute, wearable at 8
    14 to 17 Whisperdale Foul Beasts An amulet with +2 to everything for level 20
    18 to 22 Krog Payment in Full A cap with +2 to everything and +30 health, no requirement
    20 to 27 Silvest Gem Thieves, to its second step +3 Str, +3 Int, +3 Dex in one item, no requirement
    20 to 23 Silvest Dalvon's Undead For mages: +4 Int +5 Con, no requirement
    22 to 26 Whisperdale Termigon Armor Gloves with +3 to everything and +15 health, no requirement

    What to wear from level 8 to 25

    Put together from the rewards above. The patron's pieces come on top as your coins allow; they have no level requirement and beat most of this in your main attribute, but these give you something in every slot long before you can afford the set.

    Slot From Valinor Level 8 to 15 Level 15 to 25
    Head Helm of the Island Guard (+1 Str) Hard Leather Cap (Krog; +2 Str +1 Dex +2 Con, level 10) Shadow Helm (Krog; +2 all) or Dunaga's Cap (Krog; +2 all, +30 health)
    Neck Frump's Amulet (+1 all, +5 health) the patron's Necklace Warg Eye Amulet (Whisperdale; +2 all, level 20)
    Cloak Zombie Skin Cloak (+1 all) Shadow Cloak (Krog; +2 all)
    Chest Triddle Shell Armor (+1 Con) Worn Guard Halfplate (Whisperdale; level 15), then the chest of your own set at 20
    Hands Fire Ant Leg Bracer (Krog; +1 Con, level 8) Shadow Gloves (Krog; +1 all), then Termigon Battle Mittens (Whisperdale; +3 all, +15 health)
    Waist Supply Runner's Belt Sash of the Astari Guard (Whisperdale; +3 Dex +3 Con, level 7) the patron's Belt
    Legs Spider Silk Leggings (+1 Int, +25 health) Naturalist Greaves (Krog; mages) Shadow Leggings (Krog; +2 all)
    Feet Marsh Soaked Boots (+2 Dex +2 Con) the patron's Boots, or the boots of your own set at 20
    Ring Ring of Sozal (Silvest; +1 all, +10 health) the patron's Ring
    Off hand Lamp of Isos (Krog; +5 Soul) the patron's Guardian

    "Your own set" is the Orcish Warrior set in Krog, the Clockwork set in Silvest, the Night Elf Archer set in Whisperdale. All three are worn from level 20.

    Three good rewards compete for places already taken above. The Gemstone of Dalvon (+3 Str, Int, Dex) and the Eastwatch Holy Relic (+4 Int +5 Con), both from Silvest, are amulets, and Krog's Bloody Rag of Rumpda (+3 Con +50 health, level 15) is a ring. Slots are as AE Database lists them. It does not list the Sash of the Astari Guard, and the game's data only calls it "worn", so the waist is our reading of its name.
